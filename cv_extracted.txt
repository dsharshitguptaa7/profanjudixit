PROFESSIONAL PROFILE 
. Anju Dixit is a Professor in the Department of Physics and Material Science and Metallurgical Engineering Department UIET and 
, School of Basic Sciences, CSJMU, Kanpur, with academic experience at CSJMU since April 2007. She holds a Ph.D. in Physics 
from the University of Puerto Rico, USA, and has undertaken post -doctoral research at the University of Puerto Rico and IIT 
Kanpur. Her specialization is Condensed Matter Physics and Materials Science, with research interests in functional materials, 
ferroelectric and dielectric materials, BaTiO₃ -based ceramics, nanomaterials, energy -storage materials, thermoelectrics and 
materials characterization. Her work integrates materials synthesis with structural, thermal, surface, dielectric/electrical and 
Micro-Raman characterization. At CSJMU, she has contributed to teaching, research supervision, laboratory development, 
academic administration, examinations and academic outreach.
