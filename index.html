Spontaneous Polarization Mechanism

In the cubic phase (T > 120 °C), the centrosymmetric structure has zero net dipole moment. Switching to the ferroelectric phase displaces the central Ti⁴⁺ cation relative to the oxygen octahedron, breaking symmetry to produce a permanent macroscopic dipole moment P⃗.

Prof. Anju Dixit is a Professor in the Department of Physics and Material Science and Metallurgical Engineering Department (MSME) at UIET, School of Basic Sciences, Chhatrapati Shahu Ji Maharaj University (CSJMU), Kanpur. She has served the university with academic distinction since April 2007, and was promoted to full Professor through the Career Advancement Scheme (CAS) in 2026.

Prof. Dixit received her Ph.D. in Physics in 2004 from the University of Puerto Rico, USA, under prestigious National Science Foundation (NSF) fellowships, focusing on sol-gel derived BaZrTiO₃ thin film dielectrics. Following her doctorate, she conducted advanced postdoctoral research at the University of Puerto Rico and IIT Kanpur, subsequently receiving the competitive DST Young Scientist Fellowship to probe polar nano-regions and sublattice ordering in relaxor ferroelectric actuator systems.

Her experimental program integrates wet-chemical sol-gel, hydrothermal, and solid-state synthesis with rigorous structural (XRD), microstructural (SEM/AFM), vibrational (Micro-Raman), thermal, and broad-frequency dielectric/electrical impedance spectroscopy. Her research portfolio addresses critical societal challenges across high-permittivity electroceramics, supercapacitor electrodes, thermoelectric energy harvesting, transition-metal carbide fuel-cell catalysts, and post-quantum cryptographic hardware primitives.
